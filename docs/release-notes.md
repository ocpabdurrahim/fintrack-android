FinTrack is a daily income and expense tracker for Android. It works fully offline: your records stay on your phone.

**Install:** download `FinTrack-1.0.0.apk` below on your phone, open it, and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when Android asks. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

### What's in 1.0.0
- Record income, expenses and transfers between accounts in a few taps
- Home: total balance, this month's income and expense, today's spending
- History by month or search across everything, grouped by day
- Statistics: spending by category, six-month trend, daily spending
- Monthly budgets, overall and per category
- Accounts (cash, bank, mobile wallets) with starting balances
- Your own categories with emoji and colours
- Repeating entries (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) for rent, salary and subscriptions
- PIN lock with fingerprint unlock
- Daily reminder notification
- Backup and restore to a file, CSV export for Excel and Google Sheets
- Taka by default with 12,34,567 grouping; 20 other currencies; light and dark theme
