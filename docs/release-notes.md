FinTrack is a daily income and expense tracker for Android, with a matching web app at https://fintrack-854d7.web.app.

**Already have 1.1.0?** This version arrives by itself. Nothing to download.

**Install:** download `FinTrack-1.1.2.apk` below on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager. It installs over any earlier version and keeps your data. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

### New in 1.1.2
- **Delete your account.** Under **More → Sync**, *Delete account and cloud data* removes your FinTrack account and every record stored online. You can also erase the records on the phone in the same step. The web app has the same option under **More → Account**.

### New in 1.1.1
- The line at the bottom of **More** now says whether your records sync with the web, instead of always saying they never leave the phone.

### New in 1.1.0
- **Sync with the web.** Sign in with Google under **More → Sync**, then open https://fintrack-854d7.web.app in any browser with the same account. Both show the same data, and changes on either side reach the other in seconds.
- **Updates itself.** From now on FinTrack downloads new versions from this Releases page and installs them when you leave the app. Turn on **More → App updates → Allow FinTrack to install updates** once; on Android 12 and newer no tap is needed after that. Each download is checked against its published SHA-256 and must be signed with the same key, or it is not installed. You can switch automatic updates off in the same place.
- The phone still works fully offline. Changes made without a connection upload when you're back online.
- Updating from 1.0.0 keeps every record. The first sign-in uploads them.
- Small fixes: "Today" no longer shows "-৳0", and the "Enter an amount" message sits next to the Save button.

### Everything else
- Income, expenses and transfers between accounts; history by month with search; statistics; monthly budgets
- Accounts with starting balances; your own categories with emoji and colours; repeating entries
- PIN lock with fingerprint unlock; daily reminder; backup/restore and CSV export
- Taka by default with 12,34,567 grouping; 20 other currencies; light and dark theme
